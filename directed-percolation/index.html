THE LOCAL UPDATE RULE

○○

Each infected site transmits independently to its two neighbors at the next time step.

P(infection | k parents) = 1 − (1 − p)k

No infected parents? No new infection. All-healthy is absorbing.

WHAT TO NOTICE

Three observations, one transition

01 / BELOW

Extinction wins

In the subcritical phase, a single outbreak eventually dies. Starting with a fully infected lattice, the infected fraction falls toward zero.

02 / CRITICAL

No typical timescale

At the threshold the activity is scale-free: survival, population growth and density decay follow power laws over long intervals.

03 / ABOVE

Activity can persist

In an infinite system, a seed has a nonzero survival probability and an initially infected population approaches nonzero density.

ORDER PARAMETER

Stationary infected density

From a fully infected initial condition, the order parameter in the thermodynamic limit is

ρst = limt→∞ limL→∞ ⟨(1/L) ∑i ni(t)⟩

In the active phase, ρst ∝ (p − pc)β with β ≈ 0.2765. Limits matter: a finite stochastic system eventually absorbs for 0 < p < 1.

DYNAMICAL SCALING

What happens at criticality?

ρ(t) ∼ t−αfrom a fully active lattice
Psurv(t) ∼ t−δfrom many single-seed outbreaks
N(t) ∼ tθmean count, including extinct runs

In 1+1D: α ≈ δ ≈ 0.1595, θ ≈ 0.3137. The log–log plot overlays a theoretical slope when close to pc.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT

“Active phase” means survival is possible in the infinite system, not guaranteed in an individual realization. Finite size, limited time and statistical noise can obscure a sharp transition. This is a minimal epidemic model with one-generation infectiousness, not a realistic model of human epidemics.
